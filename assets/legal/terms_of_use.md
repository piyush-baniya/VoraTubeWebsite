## 2. Your music and local content

VoraTube reads audio from Android's MediaStore and folders you explicitly select through Android's system picker. It does not upload your audio files to a VoraTube server. You remain responsible for the legality and security of the files on your device and in folders you share with the App.

You may create playlists, import or export playlist documents, select `.lrc` lyrics, choose artwork, and use the tag editor. Playlist import only resolves entries against music already present in the local library; it does not download missing tracks. Removing a song or imported folder from VoraTube changes the App's local reference and does not delete the original file from your device.

### Tag editing and media modification

The tag editor is a local editing feature. For supported files, it creates a temporary working copy, verifies the requested metadata change, and writes the change back to the original audio file using the permissions and Android storage flow available on your device. A tag or artwork change can alter the original file.

The editor supports writing to MP3, M4A, FLAC, WAV, and Ogg Vorbis files. Opus files can be read but are not written. Temporary working copies are not backups. Keep a separate copy before editing a file if you need to preserve the original.

Playback effects such as equalizer, playback speed, crossfade or gapless transitions, volume boost, and balance are applied locally during playback. They do not re-encode or upload your music.

Lyrics and genre results may be cached on your device.

You can choose links to search for a song on YouTube, search for lyrics on the web, or open the donation page at `https://buymemomo.com/piyushbaniya`. These actions are user initiated. ### Playlists, backups, and exports

Backups are not encrypted by VoraTube and remain under your control.

You can configure automatic backups at a selected interval from 1 to 7 days. VoraTube may create an automatic backup when the saved destination is available and the relevant local state has changed. You are responsible for deleting, moving, protecting, and retaining backup files. If a backup is placed in a folder synced by another service, that service's policies apply.
